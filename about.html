About the Studio

Made by hand, close to home.

Catera's Creation is a one-person studio working in paint, graphite, and clay. Every piece is made start to finish in Gonzales, Louisiana.

The Artist

Hi, I'm Catera.

I started out drawing in the margins of notebooks and never really stopped. These days that means acrylic and oil paintings, graphite and charcoal drawings, and hand-built clay sculpture, usually more than one at a time, always finished by hand, never templated or mass-produced.

Most of my work starts with a conversation. A color someone can't stop thinking about, a pet they miss, a room that needs one more thing in it. I like working close to home, for people I can actually sit down with.

How I Work

Process, not production line.

Sketch & talk

Every piece, even a gift commission, starts with a short conversation about what it needs to say.

Build in layers

Paintings get built up in sessions, not rushed in one sitting. Sculpture gets time to dry between stages.

Finish by hand

No print runs, no automated finishing. What you see in the studio is the one and only version.
