Commission Process

How a piece comes together.

01

Inquire

Send a booking request or message with what you have in mind: a size, a subject, a budget range.

02

Consult

A short call or studio visit to nail down size, medium, timeline, and price before anything starts.

03

Deposit

A deposit secures your spot in the queue and covers materials.

04

Create

Work happens in the studio, with progress check-ins for larger commissions.

05

Reveal

Final walkthrough, balance due, and pickup or local delivery of the finished piece.
