Ask a Question

Paintings, graphite and charcoal drawings, and hand-built clay sculpture are all available for commissions. Bring a subject, a color, or simply a feeling and we can shape the idea together.

Most drawings take two to four weeks. Paintings usually take four to eight weeks, and sculpture depends on drying and firing time. A specific estimate comes with your quote.

No. Local studio visits are welcome, but commissions can also begin by email with reference images and a short conversation. Finished work is available for local pickup or delivery.

A deposit reserves your commission and covers initial materials. The remaining balance is due when the piece is complete and approved. Existing work is paid in full before pickup or shipping.

Yes. Small and medium pieces can be carefully packed and shipped. Larger work is quoted individually, with local delivery available around Gonzales, Prairieville, and Baton Rouge.
